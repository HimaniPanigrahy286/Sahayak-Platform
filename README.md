# Sahayak – AI-Powered Accessibility Platform

Sahayak is an AI-powered accessibility platform designed to help persons with disabilities easily discover government schemes, understand eligibility requirements, and access important information and resources.

## 🚀 Features

* **Scheme Explorer** – Browse government schemes and benefits relevant to persons with disabilities.
* **Eligibility Checker** – Check whether a user may be eligible for specific schemes based on their details.
* **UDID & Document Guide** – Provides simple guidance about UDID registration and required documents.
* **Knowledge Hub** – Access useful information, guides, and resources related to disability support.
* **Latest Updates** – Stay informed about new schemes, announcements, and updates.

## 🎯 Problem

Many persons with disabilities face difficulties finding relevant government schemes, understanding eligibility criteria, and knowing which documents are required to access benefits.

Sahayak brings this information together in one simple and accessible platform.

## 💡 Solution

Sahayak simplifies the process by providing:

* Easy-to-understand scheme information
* Eligibility guidance
* Document and UDID assistance
* Centralized accessibility resources
* Regular updates on relevant government initiatives

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
* **AI:** Google Gemini
* **Backend/Services:** Firebase
* **Deployment:** Vercel

## 🌟 Vision

To make government benefits and accessibility-related information easier to discover, understand, and access for persons with disabilities.
